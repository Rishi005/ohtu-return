A retrospective can be held using any technique as long as it makes the team examine their work as in look into what they did wewll, what they think could be improved and how to improvements will be taken into play. Some options of ways to hold a retrospective is eg talk about things or tasks that should be stopped, started, continued, or more/less of. Another example is noting things that made people happy, annoyed, and angry so that they can find spots for improvement. There is also a lap-based model which is described in the material. We first should choose a note-taker, then go through improvement areas from the previous retro which were supposed to be implemented. Then each person thinks for one minute about things that went well, that is then discussed. then about improvements, then about something that we should stop doing. The goal is to do this cycle twice. Then, at least three solid ideas for improvement for the next sprint are agreed. The retrospective should not take more than an hour.
